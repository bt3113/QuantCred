# Limitations

QuantCred is a statistical audit tool. It is not an investment adviser, market forecaster, broker, or signal generator.

Main limitations:

- It cannot detect missing unsuccessful trials that were not uploaded.
- It does not correct for survivorship bias in the source dataset.
- It does not verify whether transaction costs, slippage, shorting constraints, or borrow costs are realistic.
- It does not validate the original backtest engine.
- It does not prove future profitability.
- It does not replace peer review, out-of-sample testing, or live paper trading.

Use the output as a research diagnostic, not as a trading decision.
