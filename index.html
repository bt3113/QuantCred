PSR

Probability that the observed Sharpe exceeds a benchmark after accounting for sample length, skewness, and kurtosis.

DSR

Deflates Sharpe evidence by raising the benchmark hurdle when many trials or strategy variants exist.

PBO

Uses combinatorially symmetric cross-validation to estimate selection-overfitting risk in a strategy family.
